## Syed Abrar Afaque

I build software, the agent workflows that build it, and the product feel that makes it worth using. I work from Bengaluru.

I design the system and make the decisions. Coding agents write most of the code. My work is making that reliable: clear boundaries, small reviewable changes, quality gates every agent has to pass, and honesty about what the agents did versus what I decided.

That loop is the point. **AX** is how agents plan, build, review and hand off work without me re-explaining the rules every session. **UX** is whether the product feels clear on the surface someone actually uses. **DX** is whether the repo, the checks and the local toolchain stay simple enough that the next change is obvious.

### What I'm building

- **Agent OS** (private for now). The operating layer for how I work with coding agents. It started as written rules inside my product repos and grew into shared policy, reusable skills and a local CLI that hold every agent I use to the same principles.
- **[SynergySI](https://synergysi.web.app).** Multi-tenant SaaS for academies and service businesses (sports, arts, fitness, coaching). The product is built; I'm migrating it to a new organisation backend.
- **[SynergySF](https://synergysf.web.app).** An operator-managed marketing OS for Indian small businesses. The app is hosted; not every channel is live yet.
- **[Tanti Infra](https://tantiinfra.com).** Marketing site, residential cost estimator and client portal for a construction business.
- **[Seam Cricket Academy](https://www.seamcricketacademy.com).** Marketing and lead-generation site for a cricket academy in Bengaluru.

### How I work

I start with the why and the boundaries, write each change down as a small spec I can review, and hand the build to agents. I keep the judgment calls: what ships, what gets cut, and when an idea needs rethinking rather than patching. Before anything gets built, I use research and brainstorming agents to argue with the idea.

Most of the product work is Flutter, Astro, React, Tailwind and Firebase.
