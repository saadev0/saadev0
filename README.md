## Syed Abrar Afaque

Co-Founder of Fajar Synergy Tech in Bengaluru, and its only developer. I'm a systems and product architect: I build software, the agent workflows that build it, and the brands that sell it.

I design the system and make the decisions; AI coding agents write most of the code. My work is making that reliable: clear boundaries, small reviewable changes, quality gates every agent has to pass, and honesty about what the agents did versus what I decided.

### What I do

- **Product and platform architecture:** multi-tenant SaaS design, tenancy and data boundaries, planning work into shippable pieces, and turning a fuzzy business problem into a system someone can run.
- **Agentic engineering:** designing how AI agents plan, build, review and hand off work. Since November 2025 I've been evolving *Architect↔Agent OS* (private for now), my own operating layer for how I, as the architect, work with coding agents. It started as written rules inside my product repos and grew into a shared policy, reusable skills and a local CLI that hold every agent I use to the same principles and quality gates.
- **Open-source and local tooling:** I reach for open-source models, MCP servers and self-hosted tools first, and get the most out of the hardware and subscriptions already on my desk.
- **Infrastructure roots:** earned the Red Hat Certified System Administrator (RHCSA) credential and spent years in enterprise tech support and operations before I moved into building products.
- **Marketing and art direction:** marketing strategy for construction and real estate businesses, and art direction for campaigns, ads and generative visuals. How a product is found and how it feels are part of the build.

### How I work

I start with the why and the boundaries, write each change down as a small spec I can review, and hand the build to agents. I keep the judgment calls: what ships, what gets cut, and when an idea needs rethinking rather than patching. Before anything gets built, I use research and brainstorming agents to argue with the idea.
